Top 10 anime rankings

Best Anime of 2026

Get ready to discover the best anime of 2026, packed with epic battles, hilarious moments, thrilling adventures, mysterious worlds, emotional stories, and unforgettable characters. Whether you love intense action, deep storytelling, or heartfelt friendships, this list has something for every anime fan.

Why fans love this ranking: This list highlights the most influential, emotional, and unforgettable anime series based on story quality, character arcs, fan impact, and action-packed storytelling.

Top anime list

  1. 10.Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

    Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood follows Edward and Alphonse Elric as they search for the Philosopher's Stone to restore their bodies. Their journey uncovers a country-wide conspiracy, forcing them to confront ambition, sacrifice, and the cost of power. With rich character development, detailed world-building, and a satisfying ending, it stands as one of anime's greatest adventures.

    Watch Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

  2. 9.Death Note

    Death Note follows brilliant student Light Yagami after he finds a notebook that can kill anyone whose name is written inside it. As he tries to reshape the world, he faces off against the mysterious detective L in a tense battle of intellect, morality, and power. The series is packed with suspense, mind games, and unforgettable twists.

    Watch Death Note

  3. 8.Hunter × Hunter

    Hunter × Hunter follows Gon Freecss as he becomes a Hunter and searches for his missing father. The story begins as a colorful adventure but evolves into a deeper tale filled with strategic battles, dramatic character arcs, and morally complex decisions. With its creative power system and unforgettable arcs, it has become a favorite among anime fans worldwide.

    Watch Hunter × Hunter

  4. 7.Frieren: Beyond Journey's End

    Frieren follows an elf mage who embarks on a reflective journey after the Demon King is defeated. As time passes and humans age, she begins to realize the emotional weight of memories, friendship, and the shortness of life. Its calm pacing, thoughtful storytelling, and beautiful emotional depth make it one of the most acclaimed series in recent years.

    Watch Frieren

  5. 6.Demon Slayer

    Demon Slayer follows Tanjiro Kamado as he searches for a way to turn his demon sister Nezuko back into a human. The series blends emotional storytelling, stunning animation, and intense sword fights into a visually powerful adventure. Its heartfelt family themes and breathtaking combat scenes helped it become one of the biggest anime hits in recent years.

    Watch Demon Slayer

  6. 5.Dragon Ball / Dragon Ball Z

    Dragon Ball follows Goku from childhood adventures to cosmic battles that decide the fate of entire worlds. It helped define battle anime with unforgettable transformations, training arcs, rivalries, and iconic techniques like the Kamehameha. The series remains one of the most influential anime franchises ever created.

    Watch Dragon Ball

  7. 4.Jujutsu Kaisen

    Jujutsu Kaisen follows Yuji Itadori after he becomes the vessel of Ryomen Sukuna, the fearsome King of Curses. It combines supernatural horror, explosively animated fights, and a cast of memorable characters like Gojo, Megumi, and Yuta. The series is known for its fast pace, stylish battles, and intense emotional stakes.

    Watch Jujutsu Kaisen

  8. 3.Naruto / Naruto Shippuden

    Naruto tells the story of Naruto Uzumaki, a lonely young ninja who dreams of becoming the leader of his village. As he grows, he builds friendships, faces powerful enemies, and learns what it means to protect people who have lost hope. Its emotional arcs, iconic battles, and inspiring message make it a worldwide favorite.

    Watch Naruto

  9. 2.Attack on Titan

    Attack on Titan begins with humanity fighting for survival against giant man-eating Titans, but it grows into a much deeper story about war, freedom, identity, and revenge. With its shocking twists, powerful soundtrack, and layered plot, the series has become one of the defining modern anime masterpieces.

    Watch Attack on Titan

  10. 1.One Piece

    One Piece follows Monkey D. Luffy and his pirate crew as they search for the legendary One Piece treasure. The series is known for its imaginative world-building, lovable characters, emotional backstories, and memorable adventures. Even with its long run, it remains one of the most beloved and ambitious anime stories ever told.

    Watch One Piece
